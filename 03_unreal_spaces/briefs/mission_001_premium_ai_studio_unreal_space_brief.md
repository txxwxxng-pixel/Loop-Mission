# Mission 001 - Premium AI Studio Unreal Space Brief

## Mission

Loop Mission Premium AI Studio Unreal Space Concept

## Core Spatial Concept

Create a premium AI studio operations room that turns the Loop Mission production system into a physical 3D environment. The room should feel like a solo operator's cinematic production studio: one central mission control table, five clear production loop zones, and a QA/archive wall that proves every output is validated and ready for handoff.

This is not a sci-fi command center, hacker room, or brand-logo set. It is a realistic, high-end creative operations room suitable for future Unreal 5 blockout, lighting tests, and cinematic renders.

## Source Direction

Use the reviewed Mission 001 ads, photoreal prompt, and landing page files as references for:

- Five production loops.
- Mission 001 card as the central visual anchor.
- Matte graphite, brushed metal, white illuminated surfaces, and deep red accents.
- QA checklist and archive manifest as trust-building proof.
- Cinematic editorial realism.

## Room Program

- Central Mission Control table.
- Five production loop zones.

- Entry reveal path.

## Five Production Loop Zones

1. Interactive Web: landing page layouts, responsive frames, deploy notes.
2. Premium Ads: campaign board, carousel cards, caption/prompt panels.
3. Unreal Spaces: blockout screen, scene plan, render camera map.
4. Photoreal Prompts: prompt cards, image crop references, QC panel.
5. Unity/Game Commerce: core loop board, monetization cards, store asset mock zones.

## Central Mission Control Table

The central table holds:

- Mission 001 card.
- Five loop lane markers.
- QA checklist.
- Archive manifest.
- Handoff arrows to next loops.

The table should be the main gameplay/readability anchor in the room.

## QA And Archive Zone

Place this on the rear or side feature wall:

- Completed mission archive shelves or panels.
- QA checklist display.
- Manifest wall with file/path-like structure.
- Deep red status marks for validated outputs.

## Lighting Direction

- Soft cinematic key light over the Mission Control table.
- Low, warm-white practical lights along the production zones.
- Subtle deep red accent strips for active mission status.
- Soft monitor glow, but restrained.
- Avoid heavy neon, holograms, or fantasy lighting.

## Material Direction

- Brushed metal desk edges and rails.
- White illuminated table surfaces.
- Frosted glass panels for production lanes.
- Deep red accent tabs, strips, and validation marks.
- Tactile paper/cards for mission artifacts.
