## Failure Analysis Guide

### Fail: Generic Office

Cause:

- Prompt is too broad or office-like.

Correction:

```text
Add central Mission 001 card, five production loop lanes, QA checklist, archive manifest, premium founder-operated creative studio.
```

### Fail: Too Sci-Fi

Cause:

- Generator overemphasizes AI or futuristic language.

Correction:

```text
Use realistic paper, matte desk, brushed metal, restrained monitor glow, editorial photography, practical studio tools.
```

### Fail: Unreadable Text

Cause:

- Too many labels or screen details.

Correction:

```text
Use minimal abstract interface shapes, simple mission cards, no readable small text, clean spacing.
```

### Fail: Not Premium Enough

Cause:

- Flat lighting or generic desk objects.

Correction:

```text
Add controlled contrast, tactile materials, graphite black and white palette, subtle deep red accent, high-end editorial campaign photography.
```

### Fail: Cross-Loop Confusion

Cause:

- Visual does not communicate five production lines.

Correction:

```text
Use five distinct production cards or lanes connected by one central Mission 001 card.
```

## Recommended Retry Order

1. Fix composition.
2. Fix lighting.
3. Fix materials.
4. Fix loop readability.
5. Fix crop-specific safe space.
