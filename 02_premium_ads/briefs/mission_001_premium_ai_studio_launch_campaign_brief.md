# Mission 001 - Premium AI Studio Launch Campaign Brief

## Campaign Title

Loop Mission: Build The Studio Loop

## Target Loop

`02_premium_ads/`

## Objective

Create the first premium advertising campaign brief for Loop Mission itself. This mission defines the campaign direction that later tasks can turn into Instagram posts, generated ad images, an interactive landing page, Unreal space concept art, and Unity/game commerce expansion.

## Core Offer

Loop Mission helps one person run a practical AI studio by turning ideas into repeatable production missions, validated outputs, archives, and next-step handoffs across five production loops.

## Target Audience

- Solo founders building AI-assisted products or content systems.
- Independent creators who need repeatable creative production.
- Designers, developers, and makers who want structured Codex workflows.
- Small studio operators who need campaign, web, image, 3D, and game planning without a large team.

## Brand Positioning

Loop Mission is positioned as a premium one-person AI studio operating system. It is not a dashboard, generic productivity tool, or content calendar. It is a production discipline: one clear mission, one output loop, one validated handoff at a time.

## Visual Direction

- Premium studio control desk with layered production boards.
- Cinematic workspace showing five production lines as elegant stations.
- Clean black, graphite, white, brushed metal, deep red accent, and controlled neon highlights.
- Realistic screens showing briefs, prompts, campaign cards, archive manifests, and branch/worktree labels.
- Human-scale, one-person studio mood rather than corporate command center.
- High contrast, editorial lighting, polished surfaces, tactile notebooks, and precise labels.
