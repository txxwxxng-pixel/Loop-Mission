## 1:1 Instagram Feed Direction

- Composition: centered Mission 001 card with five loop cards around it.
- Safe text area: top 20 percent and bottom 15 percent should stay visually clean.
- Use case: single-image post introducing Loop Mission.
- Crop priority: central mission card, premium desk texture, five-loop structure.
- Avoid small text that must be read to understand the image.

## 4:5 Premium Carousel Direction

- Composition: vertical editorial crop with a clear headline zone.
- Slide 1: hero studio desk and campaign hook.
- Slide 2: idea chaos vs mission clarity.
- Slide 3: mission brief close-up.
- Slide 4: five production lanes.
- Slide 5: Codex inside one loop.
- Slide 6: QA and archive proof.
- Slide 7: final Mission 001 CTA card.
- Keep visual rhythm consistent: black/graphite base, white cards, deep red accent.

## 9:16 Short-Form Cover Direction

- Composition: vertical desk-to-screen shot with Mission 001 centered.
- Top safe area: headline overlay.
- Middle: mission card and five loop indicators.
- Bottom safe area: CTA overlay.
- Use case: Reel cover and first frame.
- Avoid tiny UI details because mobile preview is small.

## QC Checklist

- [ ] Image reads as a premium solo AI studio, not a generic office.
- [ ] Mission 001 is the focal point.
- [ ] Five production loops are visually implied without clutter.
- [ ] Composition leaves safe space for Instagram text overlay.
- [ ] No unlicensed logos, recognizable third-party UI, or vendor assets.
- [ ] No robot mascot or fantasy AI trope.
- [ ] Lighting feels cinematic but realistic.
- [ ] Materials look tactile and high-end.
- [ ] Prompt output can support the campaign captions.
- [ ] 1:1, 4:5, and 9:16 crops remain usable.

## Failure Correction Guide

### If The Image Looks Like A Generic Office

Add:

```text
premium founder-operated creative studio, mission cards, production loop lanes, QA checklist, archive manifest, cinematic editorial desk composition
```

Remove or reduce:

```text
office workers, corporate meeting room, generic laptop workspace
```

### If The Image Is Too Sci-Fi

Add:

```text
realistic desk materials, tactile paper, practical studio tools, restrained lighting, editorial photography
```

Remove or reduce:

```text
spaceship, hologram, fantasy control room, glowing neon walls
```

### If Text Is Unreadable Or Messy

Add:

```text
minimal abstract interface shapes, no readable small text, clean cards with simple marks
```

Remove or reduce:

```text
dense UI, text-heavy screens, many labels
```

### If The Image Feels Cheap Or Stock-Like

Add:

```text
high-end editorial campaign photography, controlled contrast, premium materials, intentional composition, subtle deep red accents
```

Remove or reduce:

```text
stock photo smile, bright generic office, flat lighting
```

### If The Five Loops Are Not Clear

Add:

```text
five distinct workflow lanes, five production cards, each lane visually separated by spacing, one central Mission 001 card connecting them
```
